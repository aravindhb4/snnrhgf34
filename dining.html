Dining

Loose, movable bench about 5′3″ (1600) × 420 deep × 450 high on legs, with a seat cushion and its own backrest, fully upholstered in one long cushion like the seat (no wood showing) (nothing fixed to the wall), so the whole bench moves as one piece. About 1′10″ clear at each end. Table 1600 × 800 (5′3″ × 2′7″), same length as the bench, rounded corners, 2 chairs opposite.
Crockery
About 1′9″ (537) wide, single glass door, glass upper 1300–2500, top storage above to the ceiling that also covers the beam. Charging socket in the open gap.
Pooja
About 2′3″ (680), right of the beam, attached to the crockery. Open gap 690–1300 (2′) with the existing switch board. Upper 1300–2500 (1200) with jaali doors, top level with the crockery. Temple top in gopuram style: 2 tapering tiers with small arch niches, a small rounded top step and a large metal kalasam; sits above the pooja in the 400 below the ceiling, tip at about 2880.

Only the pooja keeps the granite and the gap for the switch board. The charging socket moves to the pooja gap or the bench side.
Base

Only the outer corners of each run of units are rounded; where units touch they merge as one block with square joints. Handles are slim brushed aluminium bar handles (drawers, glass and pooja doors, top storage).
